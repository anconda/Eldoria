# Eldoria: Land of Magic and Fire 🎮

A text-based RPG game inspired by "The Amulet of Eldoria" and D&D, where players embark on an epic adventure in a magical realm.

## 🌟 Features

- **Character Classes**:
  - 🧙‍♂️ Mage - Master of arcane spells
  - ⚔️ Knight - Brave warrior with high defense
  - 🗡️ Rogue - Quick and cunning adventurer

- **Character Customization**:
  - 👤 Gender selection (Male, Female, Non-binary)
  - 🎭 Background selection (Noble, Commoner, Outcast, Scholar, Warrior, Rogue)
  - 💇‍♂️ Appearance customization (hair color, eye color, skin tone, height, build)
  - 🎯 Background-specific bonuses

- **Dynamic Gameplay Systems**:
  - 🎲 Luck System - Daily luck affects various game aspects
  - ⚔️ Combat System with critical hits, dodging, and status effects
  - 🌤️ Weather effects influencing battles
  - 📈 Experience and leveling system
  - 💰 Gold and inventory management
  - 🎯 Skill tree system with passive and active abilities
  - 🛡️ Equipment system with weapon, armor, and accessory slots
  - 🔄 Combo attack system for enhanced combat
  - 🏆 Achievement system with multiple categories and rewards
  - 🔨 Crafting system with materials and recipes
  - 📚 Lore system with world history and factions

- **Rich Game World**:
  - 📚 Arcane Library
  - 🐉 Dragon's Cavern
  - 🌳 Enchanted Forest
  - 🏪 Mystic Shop
  - ⚔️ Training Grounds
  - 🗡️ Thieves' Den

- **Factions and Lore**:
  - 🏰 Council of Mages
  - ⚔️ Knights of Eldoria
  - 🗡️ Shadow Thieves
  - 🌿 Druid Circle
  - 💰 Merchant Guild
  - 🐉 Cult of the Dragon
  - 📜 World history across five eras
  - 🤝 Faction reputation system

- **Quest System**:
  - Multiple available quests
  - Quest tracking and rewards
  - Quest completion system

- **NPC Interaction**:
  - 🧙‍♂️ Master Eldric - The Archmage
  - ⚔️ Sir Galahad - Knight Commander
  - 🗡️ Shadow - Master Thief
  - 🌿 Druidess Sylva - High Druid
  - 💰 Guildmaster Goldhand - Merchant Leader
  - 🐉 Dragon Priest Malakar - Cult Leader
  - Friendship system with NPCs
  - Trading system

- **Immersive Experience**:
  - 📝 Animated text display for a more engaging narrative
  - 🎭 Status effects system (buffs, debuffs, DoT, HoT)
  - 🎨 Enhanced visual presentation with emojis and borders
  - 💾 Save/Load system with password protection

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Starting the Game**:
   ```bash
   python "Eldoria Land of Magic and Fire.py"
   ```

2. **Character Creation**:
   - Choose your name
   - Customize your character's appearance and background
   - Select your class (Mage, Knight, or Rogue)
   - Each class has unique starting stats and abilities

3. **Gameplay**:
   - Navigate through different locations
   - Complete quests
   - Battle enemies
   - Trade with NPCs
   - Manage your inventory and gold
   - Level up and improve your stats
   - Spend skill points to unlock new abilities
   - Equip items to enhance your character
   - Use combo attacks for increased effectiveness
   - Collect materials for crafting
   - Learn about the world's lore
   - Work on unlocking achievements

4. **Game Progress**:
   - Track your score
   - Complete quests
   - Gain experience and level up
   - Collect items and gold
   - Unlock new abilities and skills
   - Build relationships with factions
   - Discover world events
   - Craft powerful items
   - Unlock achievements

## 🛠️ Requirements

- Python 3.x
- Required Python packages:
  - time
  - random
  - typing
  - dataclasses
  - enum

## 🎯 Game Objectives

The game can end through various conditions:
- Reaching level 5
- Achieving a score of 500+
- Completing 3 or more quests
- Playing for 5 or more turns

## 💡 Tips

- Different classes excel in different areas - choose based on your playstyle
- Pay attention to the daily luck system
- Build relationships with NPCs for better trading options
- Use abilities strategically in combat
- Keep healing potions handy for tough battles
- Weather conditions can affect combat outcomes
- Try to discover and use combo attacks for increased effectiveness
- Equip items that complement your class and playstyle
- Spend skill points wisely to build your character
- Collect materials from defeated enemies for crafting
- Learn about the world's history to understand current events
- Build good relations with factions for better rewards
- Work on completing achievements for additional rewards
